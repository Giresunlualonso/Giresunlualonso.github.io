After progressing through Red Bull’s junior programme, Liam Lawson made his F1 race debut at the 2023 Dutch Grand Prix, replacing Daniel Ricciardo. He returned to the Racing Bulls line-up in 2024 before stepping up to Red Bull in 2025.
