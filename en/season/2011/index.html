2011 season in brief

Sebastian Vettel and Red Bull won both championships for a second consecutive year. Vettel clinched the drivers’ title with third place at the Japanese Grand Prix, with four races remaining. Red Bull secured the Constructors’ title at the Korean Grand Prix. Vettel finished the season with 11 wins and 15 pole positions from 19 races.
