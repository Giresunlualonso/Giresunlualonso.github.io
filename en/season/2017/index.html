2017 season in brief

Lewis Hamilton and Mercedes won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Hamilton clinched the drivers’ title with ninth place at the Mexican Grand Prix, two races before the end. Mercedes secured the Constructors’ title at the United States Grand Prix. The 2017 aerodynamic rules changed, and Sebastian Vettel and Ferrari challenged Hamilton during the first part of the season.
