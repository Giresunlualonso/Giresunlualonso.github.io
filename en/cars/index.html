All 11 cars: technical details, photos and performance calculated from this season's timing data. Open a car for the full assessment.

What changed for 2026?

  • Lighter and smaller: Minimum weight drops from 798 kg to 768 kg. Maximum wheelbase 3400 mm (was 3600), width 1900 mm (was 2000), floor 150 mm narrower. source
  • Less downforce, far less drag: Around 30% less downforce and 55% less drag. source
  • Half electric: The 1.6-litre V6 turbo stays but the MGU-H is gone. The combustion engine drops to about 400 kW while the MGU-K rises from 120 kW to 350 kW: roughly a 50/50 split. Up to 8.5 MJ of energy recovery per lap (about double). source
  • 100% sustainable fuel: Cars race on fully sustainable drop-in fuel. source
  • Moving wings: Front and rear wings can open and close: low-drag Straight Mode on straights, high-downforce Corner Mode in corners. source
  • Overtake Mode replaces DRS: A driver within one second of the car ahead at the detection point gets an extra 0.5 MJ of electrical energy on the next lap. Boost is driver-controlled extra power; Recharge refills the battery. source

How are the scores calculated?
  • One-lap pace: the team's best qualifying time as a percentage gap to pole, averaged over the season.
  • Race pace: median of the team's fastest driver's clean laps (excluding lap 1, pit and safety car laps), as a percentage gap to the fastest team.
  • Top speed: gap between the team's highest speed-trap reading and the best of that race (km/h).
  • Reliability: share of starts not finished (crashes and technical failures together).

Bar: best team full, weakest empty, the rest scaled by the size of the gap. Number: rank among 11 teams. Source: OpenF1 and Jolpica timing data.
