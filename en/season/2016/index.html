2016 season in brief

Nico Rosberg and Mercedes won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Rosberg finished second in the Abu Dhabi finale to beat Hamilton by five points, with the title decided in the last race. Mercedes had clinched the Constructors’ Championship at the Japanese Grand Prix, four races earlier. Rosberg announced his retirement from Formula 1 shortly after winning the title.
