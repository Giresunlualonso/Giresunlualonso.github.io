Bahrain GP in Malaysia: Max Verstappen wins ahead of Antonelli and Hamilton

4 October 2026

Max Verstappen won the Bahrain GP in Malaysia at Sepang International Circuit on 4 October 2026, taking the chequered flag after 55 laps. The Red Bull driver started from pole position.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) finished second, 2.307 seconds behind, with Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari) third, 4.919 seconds back.

The biggest mover was Arvid Lindblad, who climbed from 22nd on the grid to finish 10th (12 places).

Max Verstappen set the fastest lap of the race, a 1:38.220.

Albon and Bottas did not finish.

Verstappen started on intermediate tyres and switched to soft on lap 2, then soft on lap 3, then soft on lap 10, a 3-stop race.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli now leads the championship on 320 points, 84 ahead of George Russell.
