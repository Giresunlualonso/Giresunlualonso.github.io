Williams bring major Baku upgrade: two new chassis and a weight cut

26 September 2026

Williams ran a major package in Baku combining two new chassis, a large weight-saving programme and an aerodynamic update.

Carlos Sainz reached Q3 and finished 10th, while Alexander Albon crashed on lap 31 after a gust of wind.

Team principal James Vowles said the race pace did not match the qualifying performance and that there is work to do before Malaysia.
