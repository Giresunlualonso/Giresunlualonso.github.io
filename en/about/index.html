About us and editorial standards

F1 Telemetri is an independent fan site for people who want to follow Formula 1 through data. We have no connection with Formula 1, the FIA or any team.

Where does the data come from?

Race results, standings and past seasons (since 2000) come from the Jolpica F1 database. Lap times, tyre stints, pit stops and telemetry from 2023 onwards come from OpenF1. The site currently covers 27 seasons and 519 races. Data is refreshed through race weekends; the last update date is shown at the foot of every page.

How we write the news

  • Compiled stories are written in our own words from at least one, usually two or more, sources. The sources are listed with links under every story.
  • Every fact is checked against its source before publication. Anything we cannot find in an accessible source is not published.
  • Quotes are kept short and attributed to who said them and where. Other publishers’ articles are not copied.
  • Race and qualifying reports are generated automatically from official timing data, and say so on the page.
  • Headlines under “elsewhere” are taken from publishers’ RSS feeds; the full story is on the publisher’s site.

Calculations and estimates

Driver ratings, car performance bars, pit stop analysis and the title calculator are computed from data only; their methods are explained on those pages. Values that cannot be measured directly, such as battery (energy) management, are labelled as estimates.

Photos and videos

Photos are chosen from Wikimedia Commons, only from freely licensed files (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public domain). Every photo is credited with its photographer and licence. Videos are embedded from the official Formula 1 YouTube channel; no files are copied.

Corrections

When we find a mistake we correct the story. If official results change later because of penalties, pages show the new result after the next data update.
