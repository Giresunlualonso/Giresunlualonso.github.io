2013 season in brief

Sebastian Vettel and Red Bull won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Vettel clinched his fourth consecutive drivers’ title by winning the Indian Grand Prix, with three races remaining. Red Bull also secured the Constructors’ title in India. Vettel won the final nine races of the season.
