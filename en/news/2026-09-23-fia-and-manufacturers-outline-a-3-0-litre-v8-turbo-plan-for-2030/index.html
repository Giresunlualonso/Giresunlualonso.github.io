FIA and manufacturers outline a 3.0-litre V8 turbo plan for 2030

23 September 2026

The draft plan calls for a 3.0-litre turbo V8 that is at least 50kg lighter, with a simplified 100kW electric motor instead of today's 350kW.

A "push-to-pass" boost system is also under consideration.

A 2030 debut would end the current rules cycle one year early. A final decision needs consensus among the manufacturers.
