2001 season in brief

Michael Schumacher and Ferrari won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Schumacher clinched the drivers’ title by winning the Hungarian Grand Prix, with four races remaining. Ferrari’s consistent form also secured the teams’ crown.
