Pirelli targets more degradation in its 2027 tyres

28 September 2026

Pirelli is evaluating changes to its 2027 Formula 1 tyres that would make them lose performance more quickly. Reports published on September 28 said the aim is to increase tyre wear over a race distance and make it a greater factor in teams’ pit stop choices.

The work responds to how often a single stop has been the preferred approach in 2026. Grande Prêmio, citing The Race, reported that a one-stop strategy was considered optimal at nine of the first 15 races. Faster performance loss could make a second stop competitive at some circuits, though it cannot guarantee a particular strategy.

Pirelli chief engineer Simone Berra said the development focus was on generating more degradation rather than correcting car balance. Pirelli is examining solutions for both front and rear tyres that could increase heat generation and the rate of performance loss during a stint.

According to Grande Prêmio, Ferrari and Racing Bulls took part in a two-day development programme at Imola, with Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc completing 623 kilometres on the first day. The 2027 compounds have not been finalised, and Pirelli has a wet-weather tyre test planned at Magny-Cours in October.
