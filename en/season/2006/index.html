2006 season in brief

Fernando Alonso and Renault won both championships for a second consecutive season. Alonso secured the drivers’ title with second place at the Brazilian Grand Prix; Michael Schumacher’s engine failure in Japan proved pivotal to the contest. Ferrari and Renault fought for both titles throughout the year. Schumacher announced his first retirement from Formula 1 at the end of the season.
