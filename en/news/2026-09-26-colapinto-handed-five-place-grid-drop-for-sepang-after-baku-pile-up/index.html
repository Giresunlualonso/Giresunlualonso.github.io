Colapinto handed five-place grid drop for Sepang after Baku pile-up

26 September 2026

The stewards found Franco Colapinto wholly or predominantly to blame for the Turn 1 crash at a Safety Car restart in Baku.

Colapinto hit his teammate Pierre Gasly, whose car then hit Lando Norris; both Gasly and Norris retired.

Because Colapinto did not finish, his 10-second penalty was converted into a five-place grid drop at the next race, the Bahrain GP held at Sepang.

The stewards declined to treat it leniently as a first-lap-style incident because no other driver contributed to it.
