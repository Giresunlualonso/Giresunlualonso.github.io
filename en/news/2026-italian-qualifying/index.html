Italian GP qualifying: Pierre Gasly on pole

5 September 2026

Pierre Gasly (Alpine) took pole position for the Italian GP with a 1:21.786.

George Russell qualified second, 0.060 seconds back, with Oscar Piastri third, 0.180 seconds off pole.

The 10 drivers in Q3: Gasly, Russell, Piastri, Leclerc, Hamilton, Verstappen, Antonelli, Colapinto, Norris and Lindblad.

Knocked out in Q1: Tsunoda, Albon, Bottas, Pérez, Alonso and Stroll.

The race starts at Sunday, 6 Sep 16:00.
