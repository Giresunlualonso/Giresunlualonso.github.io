2007 season in brief

Kimi Räikkönen and Ferrari won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Räikkönen won the season-ending Brazilian Grand Prix to finish one point ahead of Lewis Hamilton; Hamilton and Fernando Alonso ended the year tied on points. Three drivers entered the final race with a chance at the title. McLaren lost its Constructors’ Championship points following the “Spygate” investigation.
