2025 season in brief

Lando Norris and McLaren won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Norris finished third in the Abu Dhabi finale to beat Max Verstappen by two points, despite Verstappen winning the race. McLaren clinched the Constructors’ title at the Singapore Grand Prix, with six races remaining. Norris, Verstappen and Oscar Piastri all entered the final race with a chance at the drivers’ title.
