Ferrari publicly back Vasseur amid Horner speculation

29 September 2026

Ferrari communications chief Maria Conti held a short-notice press call to confirm the team's backing for team principal Frédéric Vasseur.

The speculation began after Christian Horner told The Times that Ferrari is the only team he would join without a share of ownership.

Conti stressed the team's "full confidence" in Vasseur and his team.
