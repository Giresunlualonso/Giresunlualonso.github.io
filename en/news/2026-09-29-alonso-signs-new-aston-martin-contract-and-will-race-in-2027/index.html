Alonso signs new Aston Martin contract and will race in 2027

29 September 2026

Aston Martin announced on 29 September that Fernando Alonso will stay with the team for the 2027 season. The team also confirmed that Lance Stroll keeps his seat for 2027.

The length of the deal was not disclosed. Motorsport.com reported that it runs into 2027, while the BBC said Alonso will race for at least one more year.

The 45-year-old Spaniard said the main reason to continue is that he still believes he is as fast as anybody. Alonso, a critic of the 2026 hybrid rules all season, said the shift of the power split towards 60-40 by 2028 should make things a little easier.

He summed up his decision with the words "racing is my life, my passion". Aston Martin are currently last of the 11 teams this season.
