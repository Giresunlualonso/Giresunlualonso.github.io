Spanish GP: Andrea Kimi Antonelli wins ahead of Verstappen and Norris

13 September 2026

Andrea Kimi Antonelli won the Spanish GP at Madring on 13 September 2026, taking the chequered flag after 57 laps. The Mercedes driver started from 2nd.

Max Verstappen (Red Bull) finished second, 4.351 seconds behind, with Lando Norris (McLaren) third, 5.089 seconds back.

The biggest mover was Oliver Bearman, who climbed from 22nd on the grid to finish 16th (6 places).

George Russell set the fastest lap of the race, a 1:35.587.

Sainz, Pérez, Stroll and Hamilton did not finish.

Antonelli started on medium tyres and switched to hard on lap 15, a 1-stop race.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli now leads the championship on 292 points, 81 ahead of George Russell.
