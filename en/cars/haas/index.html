How are the scores calculated?
  • One-lap pace: the team's best qualifying time as a percentage gap to pole, averaged over the season.
  • Race pace: median of the team's fastest driver's clean laps (excluding lap 1, pit and safety car laps), as a percentage gap to the fastest team.
  • Top speed: gap between the team's highest speed-trap reading and the best of that race (km/h).
  • Reliability: share of starts not finished (crashes and technical failures together).

Bar: best team full, weakest empty, the rest scaled by the size of the gap. Number: rank among 11 teams. Source: OpenF1 and Jolpica timing data.
