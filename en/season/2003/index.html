2003 season in brief

Michael Schumacher and Ferrari won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Schumacher finished eighth in the Suzuka finale to secure the title by two points over Kimi Räikkönen. A new points system awarded points to the top eight drivers instead of the top six.
