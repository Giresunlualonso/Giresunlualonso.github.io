2009 season in brief

Jenson Button and Brawn GP won the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships. Button clinched the drivers’ title with a fifth place at the Brazilian Grand Prix, one race before the end. After Honda withdrew, Ross Brawn bought the team, which won both championships in its first season. The double diffuser and the team’s strong start were defining features of its campaign.
