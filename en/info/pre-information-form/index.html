4. Delivery

Orders are shipped within 1–3 business days and delivered within 30 days at the latest by Örnek Kargo. If delivery is late you may terminate the contract.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

  • You may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason and without any penalty. If several items are delivered separately, the period starts from delivery of the last item.
  • Send your withdrawal notice by email to destek@f1telemetri.com, to our registered e-mail address (ornekpitduvari@hs01.kep.tr) or in writing, quoting your order number.
  • Return the goods within 10 days of your notice free of charge with Örnek Kargo using return code F1T-IADE-2026. Returns sent with another carrier are not refunded for shipping.
  • All payments, including any delivery charge you paid, are refunded within 14 days of our receiving your withdrawal notice, using the same payment method and at no cost to you.
  • You may handle the goods as needed to inspect them. You are liable for any loss of value caused by use beyond that (washed, stained, tags removed).
  • Signed items can also be returned. They must come back with the signature undamaged and the certificate of authenticity (COA) and numbered hologram tag complete; otherwise the loss of value is deducted from the refund.
  • None of our goods are made to the customer's specification, so the withdrawal exceptions of the Regulation (art. 15) do not apply.

Exchanges

For a size exchange, state the size you want with your withdrawal notice; if in stock, the new item is sent free of charge once your return arrives. Exchanges of signed items depend on stock.

Faulty goods

If goods are faulty (manufacturing defect, wrong item, or found not to be genuine) you may choose under Law no. 6502 art. 11: withdrawal with a full refund, a price reduction, free repair or replacement. These rights may be exercised within 2 years of delivery (art. 12). We pay the shipping costs for faulty goods.

Complaints and disputes

Send complaints to destek@f1telemetri.com. Within the monetary limits set annually by the Ministry of Trade you may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee where you live or where the purchase was made, and above those limits to the Consumer Court; mandatory mediation rules apply where relevant.
