Pirelli developing higher-degradation tyres for 2027

28 September 2026

The aim is to cut down on one-stop races: nine of the 15 races in 2026 were won with a single stop.

The 2026 tyres were designed without real 2026 cars, whereas the 2027 tyres are being tested on real ones.

Ferrari and Racing Bulls completed 400 laps at Imola; the next test is at Magny-Cours in mid-October.
