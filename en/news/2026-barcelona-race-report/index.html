Barcelona GP: Lewis Hamilton wins ahead of Russell and Norris

14 June 2026

Lewis Hamilton won the Barcelona GP at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya on 14 June 2026, taking the chequered flag after 66 laps. The Ferrari driver started from 2nd.

George Russell (Mercedes) finished second, 19.561 seconds behind, with Lando Norris (McLaren) third, 23.719 seconds back.

The biggest mover was Pierre Gasly, who climbed from 14th on the grid to finish 7th (7 places).

Lewis Hamilton set the fastest lap of the race, a 1:20.122.

Albon, Alonso, Hülkenberg, Bottas and Stroll did not finish.

Hamilton started on soft tyres and switched to hard on lap 12, then medium on lap 28, then hard on lap 42, a 3-stop race.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli now leads the championship on 156 points, 41 ahead of Lewis Hamilton.
